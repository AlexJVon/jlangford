Before we meet

Helpful to have on hand

None of this is required for a first conversation, but the more you have, the more useful that first meeting will be.

  • Property address, or the neighborhood you're considering
  • A boundary survey (and tree survey, if you have one)
  • Any HOA, deed-restriction or neighborhood design guidelines
  • A rough budget and when you'd like to move in
  • A list of rooms you need — and the ones you'd love to have
  • Photos of houses or rooms you like (Houzz and Pinterest boards are great)
  • Plans from the Plan Library that caught your eye
